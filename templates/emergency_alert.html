Emergency Alert System

Medical Emergency

This system is designed for genuine medical emergencies only. Use this feature when you are experiencing:

  • Severe chest pain or difficulty breathing
  • Loss of consciousness
  • Severe bleeding
  • Signs of stroke or heart attack
  • Any life-threatening condition

If this is a true emergency, click the button below to alert hospitals and emergency services.

What happens when you trigger an emergency alert?
  • Your location and medical information are sent to nearby hospitals
  • Emergency services are notified immediately
  • Your emergency contact (if provided) is notified
  • Your medical history and current symptoms are shared with responders
